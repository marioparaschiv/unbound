Unbound's own options are on the **Unbound** page, at the top of the **Unbound** section of Discord's settings. The page shows your Unbound version at the top. Tap it to see what changed in that version on GitHub.

## Quick actions

Three buttons sit under the version.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Restart** | Restarts Discord, after asking first |
| **Updates** | Checks your addons and Unbound for updates. See [Updates](/docs/user/managing-addons#updates) |
| **Report Bug** | Opens a new bug report on GitHub, already filled in with your versions and device |

## Recovery Mode

Stops every addon from loading. Use it when an addon breaks Discord and you need to get in to remove it. Unbound asks you to restart when you flip it. Turn it off and restart again when you're done. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/user/troubleshooting).

## Account

Sign in with Discord to use [Sync](/docs/user/sync) and get [sponsor perks](/docs/user/sync#sponsor-perks).

## Preferences

<ResponseField name='Toasts'>
	Opens the settings for the small notifications that slide in at the top of the screen. See [Toasts](#toasts) below.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name='Sync'>
	Keeps your addons and settings the same on every device. See [Account and Sync](/docs/user/sync).
</ResponseField>

## Advanced

Most people never need these.

<ResponseField name='Developer Mode'>
	Off by default. Adds the **Developer** page, with tools for people who build addons, and logs extra detail. See [Debugging](/docs/developer/development/debugging).
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name='Discord Staff Settings'>
	Off by default. Shows the settings Discord keeps for its own staff. They're Discord's options, not Unbound's.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name='Send Crash Reports'>
	Off by default. When a plugin crashes, freezes or fails to start, sends a report to Unbound. Unbound asks before turning it on. [Privacy](/docs/user/privacy#what-unbound-itself-sends) lists what a report holds.
</ResponseField>

## Help

**Support Server** joins Unbound's Discord server, where you can ask for help. **Documentation** opens this site, and **GitHub** opens Unbound's code.

## Toasts

Open these from **Toasts** under **Preferences**. A preview at the top shows each change as you make it, and **Send a test toast** shows a real one.

<ResponseField name='Enable Toasts'>
	On by default. Replaces Discord's toasts with Unbound's. Turn it off to get Discord's own back. Toasts from Unbound and your plugins then don't show.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name='Animations'>
	On by default. Slides toasts in and out. Off, they appear and disappear at once.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name='Opacity'>
	How solid a toast is. **80%** by default. Below 100%, **Blur** softens what's behind it.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name='Blur'>
	How much the background behind a toast is blurred. **15%** by default.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name='Max Toasts on Screen'>
	How many toasts show at once, up to 10. **3** by default. **Unlimited** shows every toast.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name='Duration'>
	How long a toast stays, up to 10 seconds. **5s** by default. **Indefinite** keeps it until you close it. A toast that sets its own length ignores this.
</ResponseField>
