Unbound has two parts, and they update differently.

| Part | What it is | How it updates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Unbound** | The features you see in Discord's settings | On its own, every time you open Discord |
| **The app** | The Discord app or tweak you installed | By you, the same way you installed it |

Your addons update on their own too. See [Updates](/docs/user/managing-addons#updates).

## Unbound updates itself

Each time Discord starts, Unbound checks for a newer version and downloads it only if there is one. You don't need to do anything. If the check fails, for example without internet, Unbound keeps the version it already has. On Android, a dialog may say so. Tap **Okay**.

To check right away, open the [toolbox](/docs/user/troubleshooting#open-the-toolbox) and tap **Refetch Bundle**. Discord restarts on the newest version.

## Update the app

When a new version of the app is out, the **Updates** page on the **Unbound** page lists **Unbound loader** with a **Download** button. Update it the same way you installed it:

<AccordionGroup>
	<Accordion title='iOS, from a sideloading source' icon='apple'>
		Refresh Unbound's source in SideStore, Feather, AltStore or your other tool, and install the update from there.
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title='iOS, from the app file' icon='apple'>
		Download the latest [unbound.ipa](https://github.com/marioparaschiv/unbound/releases/download/loader-latest/unbound.ipa) and install it with your sideloading tool, as you did the first time.
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title='iOS, jailbroken' icon='apple'>
		Refresh your package manager's sources, install the update to the **Unbound** package, then close and reopen Discord.

		If Discord shows **This version of Discord is incompatible with this version of the Tweak.**, your Discord app is too old for the tweak. Update Discord from the App Store.
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title='Android, with Obtainium' icon='mobile'>
		Obtainium shows an update when a new build for your Discord channel is out. Tap **Update**.

		This works the same for the patched Discord and for the Xposed module. After updating the module, force stop Discord and open it again.
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title='Android, without Obtainium' icon='mobile'>
		Download the newer file from the [GitHub releases](https://github.com/marioparaschiv/unbound/releases), and install it over the old one. Use your split-APK installer for the patched Discord.
	</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Go back to an older version of Unbound

This is only on iOS. Use it when a new version breaks something you rely on.

<Steps>
	<Step title='Open the toolbox'>
		Shake your device for half a second to two seconds, or hold three fingers on the screen.
	</Step>
	<Step title='Tap Switch Bundle Version'>
		It's in the **Bundle** section, and needs internet.
	</Step>
	<Step title='Pick a version'>
		Pick a branch if it asks, then one of its last 10 builds, such as `a1b2c3d (Mar 4, 2026)`. Discord restarts on that build.
	</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
	The version you pick stays pinned, and Unbound stops updating itself. To go back to the latest version, open the toolbox, tap **Delete Bundle**, and confirm. Your addons and settings stay.
</Warning>
