Unbound is a mod for the Discord app on iPhone, iPad and Android. It lets you add **addons** to Discord: new features, new colors and new icons. You set it up once, then add and remove addons from inside Discord.

## What addons change

| Kind | What it changes | How many at once | Takes effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Plugin** | Adds a feature, or changes how part of Discord works | As many as you like | Right away |
| **Theme** | Discord's colors, sometimes with a background image | One | After a restart |
| **Icon pack** | Discord's icons | One | Right away |
| **Font** | The typeface for one kind of text, such as messages | One per kind of text | After you relaunch |

## Where Unbound lives in Discord

Open Discord's settings. Unbound adds its own section at the top of the list.

| Page | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Unbound** | Recovery Mode, updates, your account, and Unbound's own options. See [Settings](/docs/user/settings) |
| **Plugins** | Your plugins |
| **Design** | Your themes, icon packs and fonts |
| **Marketplace** | Every published addon, ready to install. See [Finding Addons](/docs/user/marketplace) |
