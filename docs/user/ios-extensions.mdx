The Unbound app file includes two extensions: **OpenInDiscord** for Safari, and a share extension. If your tool removed them, Unbound shows **Missing Safari Extension** or **Missing Share Extension** at launch; [reinstall Unbound](/docs/user/installing) to get them back.

## Enabling OpenInDiscord

<Steps>
	<Step title='Open Safari Extensions'>
		In iOS Settings, go to **Apps** > **Safari** > **Extensions**.
	</Step>
	<Step title='Turn on OpenInDiscord'>
		iOS keeps it off until you do.
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Link Handling

It opens `discord.com` and `discord.gg` links in the app: invites (`discord.gg` and `discord.com/invite`), user profiles, channels and favorites.

| You | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Tap a Discord link on a website | Opens in the app |
| Open an invite in Safari | Opens in the app |
| Open another Discord page in Safari | A banner says "Open in the Discord app". Tap **OPEN** |

## Extension Settings

Open **OpenInDiscord** from Safari's extensions menu.

| Setting | Default | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Show Open in App banner** | On | Shows the banner on Discord pages |
| **Instantly prompt to open Discord** | Off | Opens every Discord page in the app, not only invites |
| **Enable URL Types** | All on | Turn off **Discord Invites**, **User Profiles**, **Channels** or **Favorites** to keep them in Safari |
| **Discord URL Scheme** | | Leave as is unless you need another |

The banner and instant prompt can't both be off. Turning one off turns the other on.

## Passkey Login

In a sideloaded app, passkey login goes through Safari and needs OpenInDiscord on.

<Steps>
	<Step title='Start the login'>
		Discord opens its login page in Safari.
	</Step>
	<Step title='Log in'>
		A box reads "Waiting for the Discord browser session…" until you finish.
	</Step>
	<Step title='Return'>
		Safari switches back to Discord. If not, tap **RETURN TO DISCORD**.
	</Step>
</Steps>

**Safari Extension Required** means the extension is missing. **Safari Extension Disabled** means it's off; turn it on and retry.

## Sharing

Pick Discord in any app's share sheet to send up to 10 items. Photos, GIFs, videos, audio and PDFs pass through. Text and links don't.
