Unbound blocks part of Discord's analytics and, on iOS, its crash reporting. It doesn't make you invisible to Discord. Discord still needs your account, messages, and connection to work, and it still sees all of that.

### What Unbound blocks on both platforms

This part runs inside Discord on iOS and Android. It stops Discord's app from sending:

- **Usage analytics.** These are the reports Discord's app sends about what you do in it, such as screens you open and buttons you tap, including the extra details it attaches about where you were in the app.
- **Connection analytics.** These are the reports sent when the app connects to or disconnects from Discord's servers.
- **Fingerprint analytics.** Discord's app records an analytics fingerprint for your device. Unbound turns off the part of the app that handles it.

If a Discord update moves one of these, Unbound can't find it and that part isn't blocked. Unbound only notes this in its logs, so you won't see a message.

### What the iOS loader also blocks

On iOS, the loader also blocks two services built into Discord's app:

- **Sentry**, the service Discord's app uses to send crash and error reports.
- **Firebase Installations**, a Google Firebase component that registers an ID for each app install.

The Android loader doesn't block these yet.

### What Unbound doesn't block

Discord still gets everything it needs to run the app. That includes your account, your messages, the servers and channels you open, your IP address, and the device and app details Discord's app sends with its normal requests.

### Turning it off

You can't. There is no setting for the blocking, and it stays on in **Recovery Mode** too.

### What Unbound itself contacts

Unbound has no analytics of its own. **Send Crash Reports** in Unbound's general settings is off until you turn it on. When it is on and a plugin crashes, freezes or fails to start, Unbound sends `unbound.rip` the plugin's id and version, whether it is signed, how long a freeze lasted, the error message and stack for a failed start, the Unbound, loader and Discord versions, your platform, OS version and device model. It never sends your Discord account, messages or plugin settings.

It contacts these servers to download files, and doesn't attach your Discord account to these requests. Like any download, the server can see your IP address.

On both platforms, Unbound also contacts:

- **GitHub, every launch**, to update its own translations. It downloads the English text, and the text for your Discord language if that's different. The request includes your language code, like `de-DE`.
- **The link you paste when you install an addon**, plus the addon's files from wherever its author hosts them. Icon packs hosted on GitHub are downloaded through GitHub. This happens only when you install.
