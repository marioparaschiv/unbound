Unbound blocks part of Discord's tracking. It doesn't hide you from Discord: Discord still needs your account, your messages and your connection to work, and it still sees all of that.

## What Unbound blocks

On both platforms, Unbound stops Discord's app from sending:

- **Usage analytics**, the reports about what you do in the app, such as the screens you open and the buttons you tap.
- **Connection analytics**, the reports sent when the app connects to or disconnects from Discord.
- **Its analytics fingerprint**, an id Discord's app records for your device.

It also blocks third-party services built into Discord's app:

| Service | What it does | Blocked on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sentry** | Sends crash and error reports | iOS and Android |
| **Firebase Installations** | Registers an id for each install | iOS |
| **AppsFlyer** | Tracks where installs come from | Android |

There's no setting for this. It's always on, in Recovery Mode too. If a Discord update moves one of these, Unbound can't find it and that part isn't blocked. Unbound only notes this in its logs.

## What Discord still sees

Everything it needs to run the app: your account, your messages, the servers and channels you open, your IP address, and the device and app details Discord's app sends with its normal requests.

## What Unbound itself sends

Unbound has no analytics of its own. Downloads never carry your Discord account.

| When | Where | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every launch | `builds.unbound.rip` | A check for a newer version of Unbound |
| Every launch | GitHub | Unbound's translations, for English and your Discord language, such as `de` |
| When you open the Marketplace or update addons | `unbound.rip` and `cdn.unbound.rip` | The list of published addons, then the addons you install |
| When you install from a link | The link's address | The addon's files, from wherever its author hosts them |
| Only if you sign in | `unbound.rip` | Your sign-in, then your synced install links and settings. See [Account and Sync](/docs/user/sync) |
| Only if you turn on **Send Crash Reports** | `unbound.rip` | See below |

Like any download, each server can see your IP address.

**Send Crash Reports** is off until you turn it on. When a plugin then crashes, freezes or fails to start, Unbound sends the plugin's id and version, whether it's signed, how long a freeze lasted, the error for a failed start, the Unbound, loader and Discord versions, and your platform, OS version and device model. It never sends your Discord account, messages or plugin settings.

On iOS, **Switch Bundle Version** also contacts GitHub to list the builds, and **Load Custom Bundle** downloads from the address you enter.
