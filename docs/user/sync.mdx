Sync keeps your addons and settings the same on every device you sign in on. Only install links and settings are stored, never addon files.

### Where to find it

Open Discord's settings, go to **General** in the **Unbound** section, and tap **Sync**.

### Turning it on

<Steps>
	<Step title='Sign in'>
		Tap **Sign in with Discord** and approve the request on Discord's authorize screen.
	</Step>
	<Step title='Choose how to combine'>
		If another device already synced, Sync asks how to combine that setup with this device. Nothing syncs until you pick.

		- **Merge both** keeps addons from both. The cloud's settings win where they differ.
		- **Use the cloud's setup** replaces this device's addons and settings.
		- **Use this device's setup** replaces what is stored in the cloud.
	</Step>
</Steps>

After that, **Sync automatically** is on, and changes sync on their own. Tap **Sync now** to sync right away. The line under these rows shows when the last sync ran, or why it failed.

### Sync direction

| Direction | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Two-way** | Changes on any device reach every other one. |
| **Download only** | This device mirrors the cloud. Changes made here are replaced. |
| **Upload only** | The cloud mirrors this device. Changes from other devices are replaced. |

### Signing out and deleting your data

**Sign out** stops syncing on this device. Your installed addons and settings stay as they are.

**Delete cloud data** deletes your synced addons and settings from the Unbound servers and turns Sync off on this device. Nothing installed on your devices is removed.
