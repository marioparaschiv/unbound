Signing in to Unbound with your Discord account turns on **Sync** and unlocks [sponsor perks](#sponsor-perks). Sync keeps your addons and settings the same on every device you sign in on. It stores only install links and settings, never addon files.

## Turn on Sync

<Steps>
	<Step title='Sign in'>
		On the **Unbound** page, tap **Sign in with Discord**, and approve the request on Discord's authorize screen.
	</Step>
	<Step title='Open Sync'>
		It's under **Preferences** on the same page.
	</Step>
	<Step title='Choose how to combine'>
		If another device already synced, Sync asks how to combine its setup with this device. Nothing syncs until you pick.
	</Step>
</Steps>

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Merge both** | Keeps addons from both. The cloud's settings win where they differ |
| **Use the cloud's setup** | Replaces this device's addons and settings |
| **Use this device's setup** | Replaces what's stored in the cloud |

After that, **Sync automatically** is on, and changes sync on their own. Tap **Sync now** to sync right away. The line under these rows shows when the last sync ran, or why it failed.

## Sync direction

| Direction | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Two-way** | Changes on any device reach every other one |
| **Download only** | This device mirrors the cloud. Changes made here are replaced |
| **Upload only** | The cloud mirrors this device. Changes from other devices are replaced |

## Sign out or delete your data

**Sign out** stops syncing on this device. Your addons and settings stay as they are.

**Delete cloud data** deletes your synced addons and settings from Unbound's servers, and turns Sync off on this device. Nothing installed on your devices is removed.
