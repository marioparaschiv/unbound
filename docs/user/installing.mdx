Unbound comes built into a copy of Discord. Answer a few questions to get the steps for your device.

<InstallQuiz>
	<InstallResult id='ios-source' title='Install from your sideloading tool'>
		<Steps>
			<Step title='Add the Unbound source'>
				In SideStore, Feather, AltStore or your own tool, add:

				```text
				https://repo.unbound.rip/app-repo.json
				```
			</Step>
			<Step title='Install Unbound'>
				Pick **Unbound**. **Unbound (TestFlight)** is built on Discord's TestFlight beta.
			</Step>
			<Step title='Open the app'>
				Log in to Discord.
			</Step>
		</Steps>

		<Note>
			Keep the app extensions if your tool offers to remove them. See [iOS Extensions](/docs/user/ios-extensions).
		</Note>
	</InstallResult>

	<InstallResult id='ios-ipa' title='Install the app file'>
		<Steps>
			<Step title='Download the app file'>
				Get [Unbound.ipa](https://github.com/marioparaschiv/unbound/releases/download/loader-latest/Unbound.ipa) from GitHub.
			</Step>
			<Step title='Install it'>
				Open the file in your sideloading tool.
			</Step>
			<Step title='Open the app'>
				Log in to Discord.
			</Step>
		</Steps>

		<Note>
			Keep the app extensions if your tool offers to remove them. See [iOS Extensions](/docs/user/ios-extensions).
		</Note>

		<Warning>
			Don't inject Unbound into Discord yourself. It needs the ElleKit runtime, and without it the app shows a **Runtime Detection** alert.
		</Warning>
	</InstallResult>

	<InstallResult id='ios-no-tool' title='Get a sideloading tool first'>
		iOS needs a sideloading tool to install apps from outside the App Store. Set one up with its own guide:

		- [SideStore](https://sidestore.io)
		- [Feather](https://github.com/khcrysalis/Feather)
		- [AltStore](https://altstore.io)

		Then tap **Back** and pick the tool you installed.
	</InstallResult>

	<InstallResult id='ios-jailbroken' title='Install from the Unbound repo'>
		<Steps>
			<Step title='Remove other mods'>
				Unbound can't run alongside Enmity, Vendetta or Pyoncord.
			</Step>
			<Step title='Add the repo'>
				In Sileo or your package manager, add:

				```text
				https://repo.unbound.rip
				```
			</Step>
			<Step title='Install Unbound'>
				ElleKit installs with it if you don't have it.
			</Step>
			<Step title='Restart Discord'>
				Close Discord, then open it again.
			</Step>
		</Steps>

		Without the repo, open `app.unbound.ios_<version>_iphoneos-arm64.deb` from the [latest loader release](https://github.com/marioparaschiv/unbound/releases/tag/loader-latest) in your package manager.

		**This version of Discord is incompatible with this version of Unbound.** means your Discord is too old or too new, and Unbound won't load.
	</InstallResult>

	<InstallResult id='android-patched' title='Install Discord with Unbound built in'>
		Needs Android 9 or newer. [Obtainium](https://obtainium.imranr.dev) installs Unbound and tells you about updates.

		<Steps>
			<Step title='Install Obtainium'>
				Get it from [obtainium.imranr.dev](https://obtainium.imranr.dev).
			</Step>
			<Step title='Uninstall Discord'>
				Unbound's Discord is signed differently, so it can't install over the Play Store one. You'll log in again.
			</Step>
			<Step title='Add Unbound'>

			</Step>
			<Step title='Install Unbound'>
				Tap **Install** in Obtainium.
			</Step>
			<Step title='Open the app'>
				Log in to Discord.
			</Step>
		</Steps>

	<InstallResult id='android-rooted' title='Install the Xposed module'>
		On a rooted device, Unbound loads into the Discord you have. You need:

		- Android 9 or newer.
		- An Xposed framework with libxposed API 101 or newer, such as Vector or LSPosed.
		- Discord (`com.discord`) from Google Play, any channel.

		<Steps>
			<Step title='Install the module'>
				[Add the Unbound module to Obtainium](obtainium://app/%7B%22id%22%3A%22app.unbound.android%22%2C%22url%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Fmarioparaschiv%2Funbound%22%2C%22author%22%3A%22unbound-app%22%2C%22name%22%3A%22Unbound%20Loader%20%28Xposed%29%22%2C%22additionalSettings%22%3A%22%7B%5C%22apkFilterRegEx%5C%22%3A%5C%22%5Eapp%5C%5C%5C%5C.unbound%5C%5C%5C%5C.android%5C%5C%5C%5C.xposed%5C%5C%5C%5C.apk%24%5C%22%2C%5C%22filterReleaseTitlesByRegEx%5C%22%3A%5C%22%5EUnbound%20.%2B%20for%20Discord%20.%2B%2C%20Android%5C%22%2C%5C%22fallbackToOlderReleases%5C%22%3Atrue%2C%5C%22sortMethodChoice%5C%22%3A%5C%22date%5C%22%7D%22%7D) and install it. Or install `app.unbound.android.xposed.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/marioparaschiv/unbound/releases/tag/loader-latest).

				Turn on **Unbound** in your Xposed manager.

			</Step>
		</Steps>

## First Launch

Be online the first time you open Discord. Unbound downloads its latest version then, and on later launches only when a newer one is out.

Open Discord's settings. An **Unbound** section at the top means it worked.

If the first download fails, Discord opens without Unbound and shows an alert. On iOS it reads **Failed to load Unbound's bundle. Please report this to the developers.**

<Warning>
	Only install from the sources above. On iOS, a **SECURITY WARNING** alert about failed integrity verification means your copy may contain malware. Uninstall it and reinstall.
</Warning>
