## :sparkle: What it does

- :user-arrow-right: Long-press a voice channel to disconnect, server mute, unmute, deafen, undeafen or move everyone in it at once, except you.
- :circle-check: Each action asks first, and only shows when you have the permission it needs: Move Members, Mute Members or Deafen Members.
- :circle-info: Members are changed one at a time with a short pause, so a full channel does not hit Discord's rate limit.
