## :sparkle: What it does

- :clock: 🕓 after a message of yours means it's still on its way to Discord.
- :circle-check: ✓ means Discord's server acknowledged it.
- :triangle-warning: ⚠ means it failed to send.

A ✓ only says Discord has the message. It never says whether anyone has read it.

## :gear: Settings

- **Mark all my messages** also marks older messages and ones from your other devices. Off by default, so only what you send from this device gets a ✓.
