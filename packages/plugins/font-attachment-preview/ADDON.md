## :sparkle: What it does

- :text: Long-press a message with a font attachment and pick **Preview Font** to see it in three sizes.
- :pen: Type your own sample text; every font on the message updates.
- :paperclip: TrueType, OpenType and WOFF files work. WOFF2 can't be read on the phone yet.
