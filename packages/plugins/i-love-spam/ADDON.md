## :sparkle: What it does

- :msg: Messages from accounts Discord flags as likely spammers show like any other message instead of being hidden.
- :circle-info: Blocked and ignored users stay hidden; this only covers Discord's spam guess.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's iLoveSpam.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off to go back to how Discord behaves.
