## :sparkle: What it does

- :clock: Adds your own durations to the Clear after list when you set a custom status.
- :gear: Starts with 15 minutes, 2, 8 and 12 hours, 3 days and a week; change them in the plugin's settings, like `15m, 8h, 3d, 2w`.
- :text: Each option shows when it clears, in Discord's own style, and the list stays ordered by length.
