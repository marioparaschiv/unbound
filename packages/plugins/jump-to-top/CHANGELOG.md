# Changelog

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added

- A Jump to top row in the long-press sheets of channels, threads and forum posts opens them at their first message.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-14

### Changed

- The arrow moved from the channel header to above Discord's jump-to-present arrow, and shows only while that one does.

### Fixed

- Jumping now lands on the channel's first message instead of somewhere near it.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added

- Initial release, ported from Vencord's JumpToTop.
