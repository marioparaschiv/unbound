## :sparkle: What it does

- :pointer: An up arrow sits above Discord's jump-to-present arrow while you scroll back, and jumps to the very first message.
- :hand-pointer: Long-press a channel, thread or forum post and pick **Jump to top** to open it at its first message.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's JumpToTop.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off to go back to how Discord behaves.
