## :sparkle: What it does

- :sparkle: Your profile shows an activity you write yourself, such as Playing or Watching, with details, a state and an elapsed timer.
- :photo: Add a large and small image by asset key or link, each with hover text.
- :link: Add up to two buttons that open links.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's CustomRPC.

## :gear: Settings

- **Application ID** and **Name:** the name shows on your profile; the ID is needed for images from a Discord app.
- **Type**, **Details** and **State:** what the activity says.
- **Timer:** none, since the plugin started, or since midnight.
- **Images** and **Buttons:** optional extras.
