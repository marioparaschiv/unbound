## :sparkle: What it does

- :keyboard: While it is on, nobody sees "is typing" when you type.
- :eye-slash: A button in the chat input turns it on and off; the eye is crossed out while your typing is hidden.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's SilentTyping.

## :gear: Settings

- **Show button:** shows the toggle in the chat input. With it off, your typing is always hidden.
