## :sparkle: What it does

- :gift: Removes the gift button from the chat input.
- :headphones: Removes the microphone that records voice messages.
- :video: Removes the voice and video call buttons from DM headers and the call button from profiles.
- :circle-info: Each button has its own switch, so you only hide what you never use.

## :gear: Settings

- **Gift button:** on by default, as before.
- **Voice message button**, **Voice call buttons** and **Video call buttons:** off by default.
