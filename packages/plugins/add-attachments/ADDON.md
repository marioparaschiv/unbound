Add Attachments lets you add files to a message you already sent. It is a port of Vencord's AddAttachments.

There are two ways to add attachments:

- **While editing a message:** pick a photo, video or file from the chat input's attachment menu, take a picture, or paste an image. Instead of waiting in the attachment tray for your next message, the files are uploaded and added to the message you are editing.
- **From the long-press menu:** choose **Add Attachments** on one of your messages to pick photos and videos.

The plugin keeps the message's existing attachments and shows a notice while it uploads and when it is done.

Attachments can be added to your own messages and replies, but not to voice messages. A message holds at most 10 attachments, and in a server you need permission to send messages and attach files in that channel.
