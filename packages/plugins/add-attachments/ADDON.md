## :sparkle: What it does

- :pen: While you edit a message, pick a photo, video or file, take a picture, or paste an image, and it goes onto that message.
- :paperclip: **Add Attachments** in the long-press menu of your own message picks photos and videos for it.
- :files: The message keeps its existing attachments, up to 10 in total.
- :bell: A notice shows while it uploads and when it's done.
- :shield: Works on your own messages and replies, not voice messages. In a server you need permission to send messages and attach files.

## :gear: Settings

None. It works as soon as you turn it on.
