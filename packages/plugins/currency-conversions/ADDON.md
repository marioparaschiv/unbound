## :sparkle: What it does

- :shop: Prices in messages get an approximate amount in your currency next to them, like `$25 (≈ €22.62)`.
- :globe: It reads symbols and codes before or after the amount: `$25`, `12,99 €`, `£3.50`, `CHF 35`, `40 usd`, `A$20` and `$2.5k`.
- :eye: Only you see the conversions; the message itself stays the same, and code and links are left alone.

## :gear: Settings

- **Convert to**: your phone's region's currency, or any of the 30 currencies the European Central Bank publishes.

Rates come from the European Central Bank through Frankfurter, refreshed at most twice a day. A plain `$` counts as US dollars.
