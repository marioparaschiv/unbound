## :sparkle: What it does

- :msg-smile: Discord already reacts to the last message when you send `+:emoji:`. With this plugin, each extra plus goes one message further back: `++:fire:` reacts to the second-last message, `+++:fire:` to the third.
- :circle-check: Works with any emoji you can use, custom ones included, and sends nothing to the chat.
