## :sparkle: What it does

- :sparkle: Avatars in chat and on profiles show the decoration a user picked on Decor, the same decorations Vencord users see.
- :circle-info: Set your own decoration from Vencord or at decor.fieryflames.dev.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's Decor.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off to go back to how Discord behaves.
