## :sparkle: What it does

- :sparkle: `/minesweeper` sends a board of spoiler tiles. Tap a tile to reveal a number or a mine.
- :eye: One tile with no mines around it starts uncovered, so there is always a safe first move.

## :bullet-list: Options

- **size**: tiles per side, from 5 to 10. 8 when left out.
- **mines**: how many mines to hide. About one tile in six when left out.
