## :sparkle: What it does

- :paint-palette: Every author gets a name color worked out from their user id, the same on every device.
- :sparkle: The colors match Vencord's IrcColors with the same seed, lightness and saturation.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's IrcColors.

## :gear: Settings

- **Lightness** and **Saturation:** tune the colors if they look too light, dark or dull.
- **Seed:** any text here shuffles everyone's color.
- **Only users without a role color:** keeps role colors where a member has one.
- **Only in DMs:** leaves servers alone.
