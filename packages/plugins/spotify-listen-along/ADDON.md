## :sparkle: What it does

- :headphones: A headphones button next to **Play on Spotify** on someone's Spotify activity plays the same track at the same position on your phone, then follows them: next tracks, skips and seeks, and pausing when they pause.
- :circle-xmark: Tap it again to stop listening along. If Spotify isn't open, it opens Spotify and waits for it; it never starts music on an idle speaker or computer.
- :circle-xmark: The button is dimmed on your own activity, or when no Spotify account is connected.
- :music: While you play something, the **+** menu next to the message box gets a **Spotify** button that invites the channel to listen along, sending what you typed as the message.
- :lock: Needs a Spotify account connected to Discord and the Spotify app on your device. Spotify only allows it with Premium.
- :code-fork: A port of the Add Listen Along and Send Spotify invite modules of nexpid's NexxUtils.

## :gear: Settings

None. The buttons show up wherever they can work.
