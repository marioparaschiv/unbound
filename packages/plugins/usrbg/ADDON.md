## :sparkle: What it does

- :photo: Profiles show the banner a user set on USRBG, the same community banners Vencord shows.
- :circle-info: Get your own at github.com/AutumnVN/usrbg.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's USRBG.

## :gear: Settings

- **Prefer Nitro banners:** when a user has both, show their Nitro banner.
