## :sparkle: What it does

- :clock: Wrap a time in backticks, such as `` `17:30` `` or `` `5:30 PM` ``, and it is sent as a timestamp that shows in each reader’s own time zone. A time already past today means tomorrow.
- :command: `/timestamp` sends any date and time as a timestamp in the style you pick, such as relative ("in 2 hours") or full date.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's SendTimestamps.

## :gear: Settings

- **Replace times in messages:** turns backticked times into timestamps when you send.
