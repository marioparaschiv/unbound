## :sparkle: What it does

- :volume-up: Says it out loud when someone joins, leaves or moves through your voice channel, like "alice joined".
- :user: Also tells you when you join, leave or switch channels yourself.
- :headphones: Speaks in your device's language, over call audio, so you hear it without looking at the screen. Stage channels stay quiet.

## :gear: Settings

- :toggle: **Announce** turns joins, leaves, moves and your own changes on or off. All on by default.
- :slider: **Speech rate** picks slow, normal or fast.
