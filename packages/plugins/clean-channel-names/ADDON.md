## :sparkle: What it does

- :text: Channel names show as plain words: dashes become spaces, emoji and decorative separators go, and fancy letters become plain ones.
- :pencil: A channel's settings still show its real name, so editing it never saves the cleaned one.
- :code-fork: After Equicord's CleanChannelName and the Dashless plugin for Vendetta.

## :gear: Settings

- **Dashes as spaces:** show text channel names with spaces between words.
- **Keep original names:** channels that keep their names as their server set them.
