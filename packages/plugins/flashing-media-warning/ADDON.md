## :sparkle: What it does

- :eye: Long-press a message with a GIF or video and pick **Check for Flashing**: its frames are read without playing it.
- :shield: Flags rapid flashing as WCAG defines it: more than three large brightness swings within one second over a quarter of the picture.
- :photo: A brightness timeline and frame-by-frame stepping show what was found, starting at the busiest second.

## :gear: Settings

None. Videos are checked for their first 15 seconds.
