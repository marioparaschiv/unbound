## :sparkle: What it does

- :music: Spotify and Deezer song, album and playlist embeds, Apple Music song and album embeds and Tidal song embeds get a player inside them, like desktop. Tap play to hear a 30-second preview without leaving the chat.
- :magnifier: Songs Spotify, Deezer or Tidal have no clip for are matched on iTunes by title and artist, as TrackDuel does.
- :share-up-right: Tapping the link still opens the music app.
- :code-fork: After fres's Spotify Preview for Vendetta.
