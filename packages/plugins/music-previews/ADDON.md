## :sparkle: What it does

- :music: Tapping a Spotify song, album or playlist link, or an Apple Music song or album link, opens a sheet that plays 30-second previews without leaving Discord.
- :magnifier: Songs Spotify has no clip for are matched on iTunes by title and artist, as TrackDuel does.
- :share-up-right: **Open in Spotify** or **Open in Apple Music** in the sheet still takes you to the app.
- :code-fork: After fres's Spotify Preview for Vendetta.
