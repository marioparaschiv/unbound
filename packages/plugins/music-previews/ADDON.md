## :sparkle: What it does

- :music: Spotify song, album and playlist embeds and Apple Music song and album embeds get a player inside them, like desktop. Tap play to hear a 30-second preview without leaving the chat.
- :magnifier: Songs Spotify has no clip for are matched on iTunes by title and artist, as TrackDuel does.
- :share-up-right: Tapping the link still opens Spotify or Apple Music.
- :code-fork: After fres's Spotify Preview for Vendetta.
