## :sparkle: What it does

- :text: Shows length over your limit, like `120/2000`, while you type.
- :star: The limit is 4000 characters with Nitro and 2000 without.
- :eye: Hides while the chat input is empty and never changes its layout.
- :arrow-up: Moves up to clear the slowmode notice in channels that have one.

## :gear: Settings

- :color-palette: **Color thresholds** turns the counter yellow at 50% of the limit, orange at 75% and red at 90%. Off, it stays in Discord's brand color. On by default.
