## :sparkle: What it does

- :bell: Calls from users and group DMs you pick don't ring on this device; you can still join from the chat.
- :msg: Their messages arrive and notify as usual. Nobody is blocked on Discord.
- :users: Everyone else in a silenced call still rings.

## :gear: Settings

- **Silenced users**: their DM calls stay quiet.
- **Silenced group DMs**: calls in these groups stay quiet.
