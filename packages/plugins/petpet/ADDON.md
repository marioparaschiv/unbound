## :sparkle: What it does

- :heart: `/petpet` and a user turns their avatar into a GIF of a hand patting it, and adds it to your message.
- :msg: Long-press a message to **Pet** its author, or **Pet This Image** when it has a picture.
- :photo: The GIF has a transparent background, so it sits nicely in chat. Nothing is sent until you send it.
