# Changelog

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-10

### Changed

- The settings are drawn by Unbound's settings panel, with every setting under one heading. Your saved notification level carries over, and the plugin works the same as before.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- The settings screen, the menu row and the confirmation toast can now be translated. Your saved settings stay as they are, and the plugin works the same as before.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added

- Initial release, ported from Vencord's NewGuildSettings.
