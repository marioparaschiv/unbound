## :sparkle: What it does

- :palette: Role colors on names in chat move toward colors you can tell apart, for protanopia, deuteranopia or tritanopia.
- :at-sign: Role mentions get the same adjustment.
- :sparkle: Colors that already read well barely change, so everyone else's chat looks the same.

## :gear: Settings

- **Color Vision:** the kind of color blindness to adjust for.
- **Strength:** how far colors move.
- **Role Mentions:** turn off to leave mention colors alone.
