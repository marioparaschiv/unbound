## :sparkle: What it does

- :circle-user: User mentions show the user's avatar, cropped to a circle, in front of the name.
- :shield-user: Role mentions show the role's icon after the name.
- :palette: On iOS, a role without a custom icon gets a two-person symbol in the role's color, or the normal text color if it has none.
- :mobile: Works on iOS and Android. On Android, roles without a custom icon and users with Discord's default avatar get no image.
- :photo: Avatars and role icons download from Discord's image servers as 32-pixel PNGs.

## :gear: Settings

None. Mentions get their pictures on their own.
