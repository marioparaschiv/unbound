# Changelog

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-16

### Changed

- The sprout is desktop's icon, a heart over a sprout in Discord's green for the theme, instead of the 🌱 emoji.
- Tapping it opens a tooltip saying "I'm new here, say hi!", desktop's words in your language.
- It follows desktop's rule: a week after joining, never for bots, people who rejoined or servers younger than a week. The days setting is gone.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added

- A sprout beside the names of people who joined the server in the last week, ported from Discord's desktop app.
