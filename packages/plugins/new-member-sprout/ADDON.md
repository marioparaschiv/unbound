## :sparkle: What it does

- :star: People who joined the server in the last week get a sprout beside their name in chat, like in Discord's desktop app.
- :speech_balloon: The sprout says "Hey, I'm new here!" when tapped and to screen readers.

## :gear: Settings

- **Days counted as new**: how long someone keeps the sprout after joining. Desktop uses 7.
- **Show over role icons**: shows the sprout in place of a new member's role icon, which otherwise takes its spot.
