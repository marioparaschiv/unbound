## :sparkle: What it does

- :star: People who joined the server in the last week get desktop's sprout beside their name in chat, in Discord's green.
- :msg: Tapping it opens a tooltip saying "I'm new here, say hi!", as on desktop.
- :shield: Like desktop, bots, people who rejoined and servers younger than a week get none.

## :gear: Settings

- **Show over role icons**: shows the sprout in place of a new member's role icon, which otherwise takes its spot.
