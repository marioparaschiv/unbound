## :sparkle: What it does

- :shield: Tap a link to Never Gonna Give You Up and you're asked first, with **Open Anyway** and **Don't Open**.
- :link: Short links like bit.ly are followed to where they lead before you're asked.
- :magnifier: Reuploads are caught by their YouTube title, not only the known videos.
- :clock: Other links open straight away; a check that can't finish in a few seconds lets the link through.
