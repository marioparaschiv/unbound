## :sparkle: What it does

- :magnifier: Adds **Search image** to the media viewer's menu when you open an image.
- :globe: Searches with Google Lens, Yandex, SauceNAO, IQDB, Bing, TinEye or ImgOps in your browser.

## :gear: Settings

- **Search engine:** ask each time, or always use one engine.
