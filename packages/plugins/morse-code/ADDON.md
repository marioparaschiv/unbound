## :sparkle: What it does

- :text: `/morse` sends your text as Morse code: `... --- ...`.
- :language: Long-press a Morse message and pick **Decode Morse** to read it.
- :circle-play: **Play Morse** sends it out as a tone, a vibration pattern or both, at the speed you pick.

## :gear: Settings

- **Play as:** sound, vibration or both. iOS vibrates in fixed pulses, so sound is clearer there.
- **Speed:** 5 to 30 words per minute.
