## :sparkle: What it does

- :lock: Emojis you can't send, such as other servers' emojis without Nitro, are left out of the emoji picker instead of showing locked.
- :sticker: Stickers you can't send in the current channel are left out of the sticker picker.
- :card_index_dividers: Server sections with nothing left to send disappear, and Favorites and Recent only keep what you can use.
- :code-fork: Replaces Hide Unusable Emojis, which moves over by itself.

## :gear: Settings

- **Hide unusable emojis:** leave emojis you can't send out of the emoji picker.
- **Hide unusable stickers:** leave stickers you can't send out of the sticker picker.
