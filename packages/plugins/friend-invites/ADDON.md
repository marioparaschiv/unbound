## :sparkle: What it does

- :user-plus: `/friend-invites create` makes a friend invite link and shows when it expires and how often it can be used.
- :msgs: `/friend-invites view` lists your active friend invites with their uses.
- :trash: `/friend-invites revoke` revokes every friend invite you made.
- :eye-slash: The results show only to you.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's FriendInvites.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off to go back to how Discord behaves.
