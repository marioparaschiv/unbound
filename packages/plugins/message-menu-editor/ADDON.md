## :sparkle: What it does

- :bullet-list: Puts the options you use most at the top of the menu you get from long-pressing a message.
- :eye-slash: Hides the options you never use, such as Apps or Create Reminder.
- :gear: In its settings, hold an option and drag it to move it, and switch it off to hide it. Options added by Discord or other plugins show up after you next open the menu.
