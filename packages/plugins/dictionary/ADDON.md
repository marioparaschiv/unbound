## :sparkle: What it does

- :book: Long-press a message and pick **Define Words** to pick one of its words and look it up, without leaving the chat.
- :text-a: Shows how it's pronounced, its definitions by part of speech, and examples. **Play Pronunciation** plays a recording from Wiktionary when there is one.
- :star: **Save to Vocabulary** keeps the word with its first definition; `/vocabulary` lists your saved words.
- :magnifier: `/define <word>` looks up any word.
- :language: English comes from the Free Dictionary API; other words come from Wiktionary, in your phone's language when it has them.
