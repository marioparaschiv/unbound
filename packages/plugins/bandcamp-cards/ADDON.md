## :sparkle: What it does

- :music: A Bandcamp album or track link's embed shows its digital price, such as **Bandcamp · $6.00**, or **Name your price**.
- :clock: Each link is looked up once a session.

## :shield: Privacy

- :globe: The price is read from the Bandcamp page itself. Nothing else is sent.
