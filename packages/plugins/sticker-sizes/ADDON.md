## :sparkle: What it does

- :sticker: Stickers in chat show at the size you pick, from half to one and a half times Discord's own.
- :circle-info: The message they're in grows or shrinks with them, so nothing overlaps.
- :code-fork: After rushii's ConfigurableStickerSizes for Aliucord.

## :gear: Settings

- **Sticker size:** 50%, 75%, 125% or 150%.
