## :sparkle: What it does

- :msg-arrow-right: Send a quick follow-up within 3 seconds of your last message and it's added to that message as an edit, so a thought split over several sends stays one message.
- :circle-check: Only merges into your own message, only while nobody else has posted after it, and never while you are replying.
- :circle-info: Change the burst length, allow merging into messages with attachments, or join with a space instead of a new line in the settings.
