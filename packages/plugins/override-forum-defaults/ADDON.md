## :sparkle: What it does

- :list: Forum channels open in the layout you pick, **List** or **Gallery**, instead of the one the server set.
- :arrow-down-wide-short: Forum posts are sorted the way you pick, **Recently Active** or **Date Posted**, instead of the server's order.
- :sliders: You can still change the layout and sort order for a single forum from its **Sort & View** sheet. Discord remembers that choice for that forum, and the sheet's **Reset to default** button goes back to your defaults.
- :image: Media channels keep their gallery, and game invite channels keep their fixed layout and order. Discord mobile only offers a layout choice in forum channels, so the plugin leaves these alone where Vencord forces your layout on them too.
- :circle-info: A changed default shows the next time you open a forum.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's OverrideForumDefaults.

## :gear: Settings

- :table-cells: **Default Layout** picks **List** or **Gallery**. List is the default.
- :clock: **Default Sort Order** picks **Recently Active** or **Date Posted**. Recently Active is the default.
