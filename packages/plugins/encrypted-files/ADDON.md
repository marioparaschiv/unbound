## :sparkle: What it does

- :shield: Turn on encryption in a DM or group with the shield button, and every file you add is encrypted so only the people there, and you, can open it. In a server channel it's encrypted for everyone whose key you saved.
- :clipboard: Share your key from the settings; long-press someone's key message and pick **Save Encryption Key** to save theirs.
- :paperclip: Long-press an encrypted `.ufx` attachment and pick **Decrypt File** to see it and save it.

## :gear: Settings

Your key and its fingerprint, and the keys you saved. Keys stay on this device; reinstalling Unbound makes a new one.
