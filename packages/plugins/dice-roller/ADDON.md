## :sparkle: What it does

- :sparkle: `/roll 2d20kh1+5` rolls dice and sends every die and the total, like `[17, ~~4~~] + 5 = 22`.
- :eye-slash: Turn on **private** to see the roll yourself without sending it.

## :bullet-list: What you can write

- `2d20`, `d%` (a hundred-sided die) and plain numbers, joined with `+` and `-`.
- `kh2` / `kl2` keep the two highest or lowest dice, `dh1` / `dl1` drop them, and `k3` keeps the three highest.
- `!` makes dice explode: each top roll adds another die.
