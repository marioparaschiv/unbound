# Changelog

## [1.4.5] - 2026-11-14

### Changed

- Credits eternal as an author.

## [1.4.4] - 2026-11-14

### Changed

- The audio file check moved to the shared plugin code. The plugin works the same as before.

## [1.4.3] - 2026-11-14

### Fixed

- On iOS the speed and download buttons sit right after the timer, with the speed sized to its label, instead of leaving a wide gap.

## [1.4.2] - 2026-11-14

### Fixed

- The download button in the iOS player is round again instead of squeezed.

## [1.4.1] - 2026-11-14

### Fixed

- The speed and download buttons stay out of audio players inside link embeds.

## [1.4.0] - 2026-11-14

### Changed

- Speed and download buttons sit inside the voice message player, on iOS and Android. Tap the speed to step through 1×, 1.5×, 2× and 0.75×; tap the arrow to save the audio.

### Removed

- The Download Voice Message and Playback Speed rows in the long-press menu; the player's buttons replace them.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added

- Long-press a voice message and tap **Playback Speed** to step through 1×, 1.5×, 2× and 0.75× without opening settings.

## [1.2.1] - 2026-11-14

### Fixed

- The store page shows its fast-forward, microphone, Android and arrow icons instead of their names.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-14

### Changed

- Renamed from Voice Messages; the old plugin moves over by itself.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added

- Playback speed: voice messages and audio files play at 0.75×, 1×, 1.5× or 2×; videos keep their speed.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added

- Initial release, after Vencord's VoiceDownload: a Download Voice Message row in a voice message's long-press menu saves its audio, to Downloads on Android and through the share sheet on iOS.
- A message of only audio files goes out as a voice message, and audio files others send can show as voice messages, ported from Custom Voice Messages for Revenge.
