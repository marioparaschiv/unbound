# Changelog

## [1.2.1] - 2026-11-14

### Fixed

- The store page shows its fast-forward, microphone, Android and arrow icons instead of their names.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-14

### Changed

- Renamed from Voice Messages; the old plugin moves over by itself.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added

- Playback speed: voice messages and audio files play at 0.75×, 1×, 1.5× or 2×; videos keep their speed.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added

- Initial release, after Vencord's VoiceDownload: a Download Voice Message row in a voice message's long-press menu saves its audio, to Downloads on Android and through the share sheet on iOS.
- A message of only audio files goes out as a voice message, and audio files others send can show as voice messages, ported from Custom Voice Messages for Revenge.
