## :sparkle: What it does

- :color-palette: Colors written in messages, such as `#5865F2`, `rgb(255, 77, 109)` or `hsl(340, 100%, 65%)`, show as pills tinted with that color, like a role mention.

## :gear: Settings

None. It works as soon as you turn it on.
