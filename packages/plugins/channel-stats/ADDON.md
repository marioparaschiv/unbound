## :sparkle: What it does

- :sparkle: `/channelstats` sums up the messages loaded in the channel you are in: the five people who wrote most and their share, the three busiest hours and about how many messages a day.
- :eye-slash: The reply shows only to you. Nothing is sent, and no extra history is downloaded.

## :bullet-list: Good to know

- It counts what Discord has loaded, so scroll up first for a longer view.
