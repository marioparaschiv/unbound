## :sparkle: What it does

- :volume-up: Replaces any of Discord's sounds, like new messages, incoming calls, mute or people joining voice, with another of Discord's sounds or a file from a link.
- :link: Links can be mp3, m4a or wav files up to 5 MB, plus ogg on Android. Each file downloads once.
- :users: Plays an extra sound only you hear when one of your chosen friends joins your voice channel. This used to be the Join Sounds plugin, and your settings carry over.

## :gear: Settings

- **Replace Discord's sounds**: pick a replacement for each sound, or a link to a file.
- **Friend join sounds**: the friends, the sound or link, its volume, and a preview.
