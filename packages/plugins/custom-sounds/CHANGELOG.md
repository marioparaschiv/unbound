# Changelog

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-22

### Fixed

- Discord no longer crashes on Android when a sound is swapped for one Discord cannot open there, such as Mute; it keeps Discord's own sound instead.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added

- Replaces Discord's sounds with another of its sounds or a file from a link.
- Absorbs Join Sounds: the friend join sound, its friends, sound and volume carry over, and the sound can now be a link too.
