## :sparkle: What it does

- :user-plus: Invites in chat show who sent them, the channel they open and when they expire, above the server card.
- :clock: Vanity and permanent invites say they never expire; dead ones say they expired.
- :code-fork: A port of BetterInvites for Equicord, by iamme and thororen.
