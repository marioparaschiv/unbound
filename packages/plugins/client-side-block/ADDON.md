## :sparkle: What it does

- :ban: Messages from blocked users don't appear in the chat at all, not even as a placeholder row.
- :see_no_evil: Messages from users you ignore, and from users you pick here, disappear too, without blocking them on Discord.
- :leftwards_arrow_with_hook: Replies to anyone it hides disappear with them.
- :repeat: The open chat rebuilds as soon as a setting changes, so you don't need to reopen it.

## :gear: Settings

- **Hidden users**: people whose messages you never want to see, chosen from your friends and servers.
- **Ignored users** and **Replies**: whether those disappear as well.
