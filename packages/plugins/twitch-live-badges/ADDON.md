## :sparkle: What it does

- :video: A Twitch channel link's embed says whether the stream is live right now, and for how long: **Twitch · 🔴 Live 2h 5m** or **Twitch · Offline**.
- :clock: Each channel is checked at most every two minutes.

## :shield: Privacy

- :globe: Channel names in links are looked up on decapi.me, which reads Twitch for you. Nothing else is sent.
