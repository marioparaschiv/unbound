## :sparkle: What it does

- :face-smile: Emoji in messages, embeds and the emoji picker draw from the pack you pick instead of Discord's.
- :circle-play: Fluent and Noto packs move where their makers animated them, on iOS and Android.
- :swap: Emoji a pack lacks, such as flags, keep Discord's look.

## :gear: Settings

- **Emoji pack:** Fluent animated, Fluent, Noto animated or Noto.
