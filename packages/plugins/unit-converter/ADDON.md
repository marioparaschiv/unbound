## :sparkle: What it does

- :ruler: Writes the converted value in brackets after measurements in chat, like `5 ft (≈ 1.52 m)` or `20 °C (≈ 68 °F)`.
- :text: Covers lengths, heights such as `5'11"`, weights, volumes, speeds and temperatures.
- :eye: Only changes what you see: the message itself, its edits and copies keep the original text. Code, links and mentions stay untouched.

## :gear: Settings

- :globe: **Convert to** picks the target: your region's units (the default), metric, imperial, or both ways.
