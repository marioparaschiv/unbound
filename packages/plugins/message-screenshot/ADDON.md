## :sparkle: What it does

- :photo: **Screenshot** in a message's long-press menu captures the message exactly as Discord draws it, with names, avatars and your theme.
- :msgs: It can include the messages right after it too, as long as they're on screen.
- :clipboard: Share the picture from the share sheet, or copy it to paste anywhere.

## :gear: Settings

- **Messages to include**: the pressed message and up to 9 after it.
- **After taking it**: share or copy.
