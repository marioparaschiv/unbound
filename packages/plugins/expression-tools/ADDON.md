Saw an emoji you need? Expression Tools adds copy, favorite and clone buttons to the sheet you get when you tap a custom emoji or sticker.

## :sparkle: What it does

- :link: **Copy URL** copies an emoji's image link at 160 pixels (GIF if animated, PNG otherwise) or a sticker's `discord.com` page.
- :code: **Copy markup** copies the text that renders the emoji in a message, like `<:name:id>`, or `<a:name:id>` when it's animated.
- :star: **Favorite** adds a sticker to your favorites, and **Unfavorite** removes it.
- :clone: **Clone** uploads a copy to one of your servers. Edit the **NAME**, search **YOUR SERVERS**, then tap one.
- :sticker: Buttons also show on the sticker sheet from a message. Unicode emojis get none.

## :gear: Settings

None. A toast confirms each action, or shows Discord's error if it fails.
