## :sparkle: What it does

- :text-size-change: Names written in styled Unicode, like 𝓯𝓪𝓷𝓬𝔂, ｗｉｄｅ, ⓒⓘⓡⓒⓛⓔⓓ or ᴛɪɴʏ ᴄᴀᴘꜱ, show as plain letters in chat and in reply previews.
- :circle-check: Names stacked with combining marks lose the clutter. Ordinary accents, as in José or Müller, stay.
- :circle-info: Only changes how names look on your device; nobody's name changes on Discord.
