## :sparkle: What it does

- :face-smile: Shows up to five faces per reaction, with a `+N` badge for the rest.
- :user-arrow-right: Tap a face to open that person's profile without touching your own reaction.
- :star: Covers normal reactions and super reactions.
- :repeat: Faces update as people add or remove reactions.

## :gear: Settings

None. Faces appear as soon as it's on.
