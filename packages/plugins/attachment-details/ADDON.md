## :sparkle: What it does

- :circle-info: Long-press a message with attachments and pick **Attachment Details** to see, for each attachment:
    - :file: its file name, type, and the type it was uploaded as when Discord converted it;
    - :ruler: its size, and the dimensions of images and videos;
    - :clock: the length of voice messages;
    - :text: its alt text, and whether it is a spoiler.
- :link: **Copy Link** copies the attachment's link.
- :circle-info: It shows what Discord reports about the file. Video length and codecs aren't reported, so they don't show.
