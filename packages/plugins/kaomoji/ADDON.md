## :sparkle: What it does

- :chat-bot: Adds `/happy`, `/smug`, `/crying`, `/angry`, `/anger`, `/joy`, `/blush`, `/confused`, `/sleeping`, `/laughing` and `/dissatisfaction`.
- :text: Each sends its kaomoji after an optional message, like Discord's `/shrug`.
- :code-fork: A port of MoreKaomoji for Vencord, by Jacob.Tm.
