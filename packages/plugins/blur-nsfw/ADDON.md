## :sparkle: What it does

- :eye-slash: Attachments in age-restricted channels and their threads show blurred, behind Discord's own spoiler cover.
- :photo: Images, GIFs and videos in link embeds there are blurred the same way.
- :pointer: Tap a blurred item to reveal it. Mobile has no hover, so a tap takes the place of hovering on desktop.
- :share-up-right: Media inside forwarded messages there is blurred too, as on desktop.
- :circle-info: Changes show on rows Discord draws after you turn a part or the plugin on or off. Reopen the channel to update the rows already on screen. Rows Discord reuses are put back as they were.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's BlurNSFW.

## :gear: Settings

Both live under **Blur**, and both are on by default.

- :paperclip: **Attachments** blurs uploaded images, GIFs and videos.
- :link: **Embeds** blurs the media in link embeds, such as GIF picker results and image links.

Vencord's **Blur Amount** setting is not here. Discord's mobile chat draws its spoiler cover natively, with a fixed blur, and has no blur strength a plugin can set.
