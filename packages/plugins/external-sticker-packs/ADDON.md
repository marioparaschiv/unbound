## :sparkle: What it does

- :sticker: A sticker button in the chat input opens your LINE sticker packs; tap a sticker to attach it to your message as an image.
- :link: Paste a LINE sticker shop link, such as `store.line.me/stickershop/product/1070`, or its number to add a pack.
- :trash: Remove a pack from the picker with its button.

Stickers are sent as plain images, so anyone sees them without the plugin.
