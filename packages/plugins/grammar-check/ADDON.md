## :sparkle: What it does

- :pen: A **Check grammar** button in the chat input sends your draft to LanguageTool and lists the spelling and grammar mistakes it finds.
- :wand-sparkle: Tap a fix to apply it, or **Fix all**.

## :shield: Privacy

- :globe: Your draft goes to LanguageTool's public API (languagetool.org) only when you tap the button.
