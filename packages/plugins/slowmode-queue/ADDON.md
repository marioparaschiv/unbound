## :sparkle: What it does

- :hourglass: While slowmode is cooling down, the send button keeps working. Your message waits and sends itself the moment the cooldown ends.
- :timer: A toast counts down the seconds. **Cancel** puts the message back in the chat input.
- :bullet-list: Several messages wait their turn and send one after another, each after its own cooldown.

Discord's own slowmode countdown stays above the chat input. Channels where you're immune to slowmode aren't affected.
