## :sparkle: What it does

- :star: A **Presets** group on the Set your status screen lists your saved statuses; tap one to set it.
- :plus: **Save current status** adds the status you have set now to your presets, and the bin on a preset removes it.
- :code-fork: A port of Equicord's StatusPresets.

## :gear: Settings

None. Presets are managed on the Set your status screen.
