## :sparkle: What it does

- :photo: When you attach photos, checks each one for hidden metadata: where it was taken, the camera, the date and the editing software.
- :map: Photos that record a location get their metadata removed before they're attached, and a toast says how many. **Details** lists what each photo held.
- :eye-slash: **Remove all metadata** also removes the camera, date and software from every photo.
- :apple: On iOS, removing re-encodes the photo as an upright JPEG with no metadata.
- :android: Android already hides photo locations from Discord, so there it removes the camera, date and other identifying tags without re-encoding, when Remove all metadata is on.

## :gear: Settings

- :map: **Remove locations**: on by default. Off, a toast only warns that a photo records its location.
- :eye-slash: **Remove all metadata**: off by default.
