## :sparkle: What it does

- :volume-up: New messages in the channel you have open are read aloud in a babbling animal voice, one blip per letter.
- :users: Each person keeps their own voice, or everyone gets the one you pick.
- :clock: Long messages are cut short and a quiet time keeps a busy chat from babbling nonstop.

## :gear: Settings

- **Voice:** one per person, or low, middle, high or squeaky for everyone.
- **Letter length**, **Volume**, **Letters per message** and **Quiet time**.
- **Your messages** and **Bot messages:** read those aloud too.
