Someone's typing in #general and now you know. Channel Typing Indicators shows who's typing right in the channel list.

## :sparkle: What it does

- :dots: Shows up to three avatars, a `+N` badge for the rest, and animated dots on channel and thread rows.
- :msg: Tap the dots or long-press an avatar for a tooltip like "Alice and Bob are typing...", in your language when Discord has it.
- :user: Tap an avatar to open that person's profile.
- :eye: Never shows you. Blocked users, ignored users and muted channels stay hidden by default.

## :gear: Settings

- :toggle: **Include current channel** shows typing in the channel you have open. On by default.
- :bell-slash: **Include muted channels** shows typing in muted channels. Off by default.
- :user-minus: **Include ignored users** shows typing from users you ignored. Off by default.
- :ban: **Include blocked users** shows typing from users you blocked. Off by default.
- :sliders-3: **Appearance** picks **Avatars and animated dots**, **Animated dots** or **Avatars**. **Avatars and animated dots** by default.
