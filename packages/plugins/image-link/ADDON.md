## :sparkle: What it does

- :link: A message that is only a link to an image or video keeps its link text above the embed.
- :circle-info: Discord normally hides that link and shows just the picture.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's ImageLink.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off to go back to how Discord behaves.
