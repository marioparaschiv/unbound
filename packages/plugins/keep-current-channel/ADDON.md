## :sparkle: What it does

- :msgs: After you switch accounts and back, or restart Discord, it opens the channel you last had open.
- :circle-info: A channel the account cannot see anymore is skipped.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's KeepCurrentChannel.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off to go back to how Discord behaves.
