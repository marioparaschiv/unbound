## :sparkle: What it does

- :speech_balloon: Tone tags such as `/j`, `/srs` and `/lh` show as small pills in messages, like Discord's timestamps.
- :book: Each pill spells out the meaning, such as `/j joking`, or shows just the tag if you prefer.

## :gear: Settings

- **Spell out meanings**: turn off to show only the tag.
- **Custom tags**: add your own as `tag=meaning`, such as `rp=Roleplay`.
