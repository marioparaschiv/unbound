## :sparkle: What it does

- :trash: **Delete Message** deletes straight away, without asking "Are you sure?" first.
- :circle-info: Every other confirmation, like leaving a server, still asks.
- :code-fork: A port of The Sun's QuickDelete for Vendetta.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off to get the confirmation back.
