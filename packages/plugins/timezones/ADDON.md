## :sparkle: What it does

- :clock: Shows a user's local time under their name on their profile.
- :msg: Adds the author's local time after a message's timestamp, when their timezone differs from yours.
- :globe: Finds timezones people shared on [TimezoneDB](https://timezone.creations.works), or one you set yourself by tapping the time on a profile.

## :gear: Settings

- **Show in messages** and **Show in profiles:** where the local time shows.
- **Show your own local time:** includes you.
- **Clock:** follow the device, or force a 12- or 24-hour clock.
- **Use TimezoneDB:** look up shared timezones. A timezone you set for someone always wins.
