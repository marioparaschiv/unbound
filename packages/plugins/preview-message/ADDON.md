Check it before the whole server does. Preview Message shows your draft exactly as it will look once sent.

## :sparkle: What it does

- :eye: Adds an eye button to the chat input once your draft has text or a staged sticker.
- :msg: Tap it to open a **Message Preview** card, rendered with Discord's own message row. Tap outside to close it.
- :link: Shows link previews for up to four links. Links in angle brackets get none, same as Discord.
- :sticker: Includes stickers staged with Sticker Preview and applies your Text Replace rules when that plugin is running.
- :globe: Link previews come from Discord's link embed service. If Discord can't resolve a link, your device downloads the page to read its preview tags.

## :gear: Settings

None. The button shows up when you start typing.
