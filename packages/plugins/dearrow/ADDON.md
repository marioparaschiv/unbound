## :sparkle: What it does

- :image: Replaces the clickbait title and thumbnail of YouTube embeds with the ones the DeArrow community voted for.
- :text: Drops the `>` markers DeArrow submitters use to keep a word's casing, so titles read naturally.
- :eye: Leaves an embed alone when DeArrow has no title or thumbnail for it, or when its top entry was voted down.
- :hand-pointer: Long-press a message with a dearrowed embed and pick **Show Original Embed** to switch it back, or **Dearrow Embed** to switch it again. Mobile embeds have no buttons of their own, so the message menu takes the place of the desktop toggle button.
- :globe: Sends the video IDs of YouTube embeds you see to `sponsor.ajay.app`, and loads replacement thumbnails from `dearrow-thumb.ajay.app`.
- :circle-info: Only the thumbnail shown in chat changes. Opening or saving it still uses YouTube's original image, as on desktop.
- :circle-info: Your switches last until Discord restarts.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's Dearrow.

## :gear: Settings

- :image: **Replace** picks **Titles and Thumbnails**, **Titles** or **Thumbnails**. Titles and thumbnails by default.
- :wand-magic-sparkles: **Dearrow by Default** replaces titles and thumbnails without asking. When off, embeds stay original until you switch them from the message menu. On by default.
- :hand-pointer: **Show Toggle** adds the switch to the message menu. On by default.
- :circle-info: Settings apply right away, with no restart.
