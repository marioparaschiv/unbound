## :sparkle: What it does

- :photo: When you attach a Live Photo, pick whether it goes as the still image, its short video, or a looping GIF.
- :video: The video is the Live Photo's own clip, fetched from iCloud when needed. The GIF is up to four seconds at 10 frames a second and 480 pixels.
- :gear: Set **Send Live Photos as** to always use one of them instead of asking.

iOS only; Android has no Live Photos.
