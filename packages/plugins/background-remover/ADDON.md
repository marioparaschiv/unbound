## :sparkle: What it does

- :wand-sparkle: Long-press a message with a picture and pick **Remove Background**. The subject is cut out and added to your message as a transparent PNG, ready to send or save.
- :apple: On iOS 17 and later, Apple's on-device subject lifting finds people, pets and objects, like holding a photo in the Photos app.
- :android: On Android there's no on-device subject model to use, so it clears plain backgrounds instead, such as product shots, logos and screenshots on one colour.

Everything happens on your phone; the picture isn't uploaded anywhere until you send it.
