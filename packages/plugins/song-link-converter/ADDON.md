## :sparkle: What it does

- :music: Tap a Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music or Deezer link and it opens a search for the same song or album in the service you pick, so you never land in an app you don't use.
- :link: Links to the service you already use open as usual, and any link it can't read opens unchanged.

## :shield: Privacy

- :globe: To read the song's name, the link is looked up on its own service: Spotify's page, Apple's iTunes lookup, YouTube's oEmbed or Deezer's API.
