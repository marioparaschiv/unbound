## :sparkle: What it does

- :msg-smile: Turns what you send into uwu speak: "really" becomes "weawwy", "hello" becomes "hewwo" and "love" becomes "wuv".
- :text: Optionally stutters the odd word (h-hewwo) and ends some sentences with a face such as owo or >w<.
- :code: Never touches code, links, mentions, channels, timestamps or custom emoji.
- :command: `/uwu` sends one message in uwu speak without turning the toggle on.

## :gear: Settings

- **Toggle:** the button in the chat input turns uwu speak on or off. It starts off.
- **Persist State:** keeps the toggle on in every channel instead of the one you set it in.
- **Auto Disable:** turns the toggle off after one message.
- **Stutter** and **Faces:** the extra flourishes.
