## :sparkle: What it does

- :circle-play: Type `/doom`, or tap **Play DOOM** in this plugin's settings, to play the DOOM shareware episode.
- :gear: It runs Cloudflare's WebAssembly port with on-screen controls. Closing the page stops the game.
