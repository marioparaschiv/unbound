## :sparkle: What it does

- :headphones: Before you join a voice or stage channel, you're asked **Join #channel?** first.
- :mobile: Starting a voice or video call in a DM or group asks too.
- :circle-info: Leaving, tapping the channel you're already in, and joins Follow Voice User or Wait For Slot make for you go through without asking.

## :gear: Settings

- **Voice channels:** ask before joining one.
- **Calls:** ask before starting a call.
