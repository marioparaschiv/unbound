## :sparkle: What it does

- :palette: Long-press a role on a profile and pick **Copy Role Color** to copy its color as a hex code.
- :users: Pick **View Members** to see the members with that role; tap one to open their profile.
- :circle-info: Large servers only list the members Discord has loaded.
- :code-fork: Ports MSMA's CopyRoleColor for Vendetta and nin0dev's InRole for Equicord.
