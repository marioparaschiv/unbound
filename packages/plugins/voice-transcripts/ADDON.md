## :sparkle: What it does

- :microphone: Long-press a voice message or audio file and pick **Transcribe** to read it as text, which you can select or copy.
- :gear: Uses the speech recognition built into your phone, on the device where it supports that.
- :text: On iOS it needs Unbound installed as an app (sideloaded); jailbreak installs cannot ask iOS for speech recognition.
