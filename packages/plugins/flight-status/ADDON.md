## :sparkle: What it does

- :globe: Long-press a message that mentions a flight number, such as **LH400** or **BA 286**, and pick **Flight status** to see whether it is in the air right now.
- :timer: Airborne flights show altitude, ground speed, heading, whether they are climbing or descending, and the aircraft type and registration.
- :link: **Open in Flightradar24** shows the route and schedule.

## :shield: Privacy

- :globe: The flight's callsign is looked up on adsb.lol, a community ADS-B network. Nothing else is sent.
