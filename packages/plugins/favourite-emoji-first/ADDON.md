## :sparkle: What it does

- :star: Typing `:` and a name lists your favourite emoji that match before any others.
- :circle-info: The suggestions keep their usual length; favourites just move to the top.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's FavoriteEmojiFirst.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off to go back to how Discord behaves.
