## :sparkle: What it does

- :at-sign: Tap an @unknown-user mention, a user Discord has not loaded, and the user is fetched and their profile opens instead of the "You don't have access to this link" notice.
- :msg: Every mention of them in the open channel then shows their name.
- :code-fork: A port of k1ng_op's ValidUser for Valenced.
