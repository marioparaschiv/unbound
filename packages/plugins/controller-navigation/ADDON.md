## :sparkle: What it does

- :arrow-up: **D-pad up / down** moves to the previous or next channel in the server.
- :arrow-right: **D-pad left / right** moves to the previous or next server, opening the channel you last had there.
- :msg: **L1 / R1** jump to the previous or next unread channel.
- :keyboard: **A** focuses the message box; **Y** marks the channel as read; **B** goes back as usual.
- :android: Android only for now.
