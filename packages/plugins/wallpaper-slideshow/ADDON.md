## :sparkle: What it does

- :photo: Rotates your chat background through a list of pictures you pick.
- :clock: Each one stays for the time you choose, from a minute to hours; **Next wallpaper** skips ahead.
- :undo: Turning the plugin off puts back the chat background you had before.

## :gear: Settings

- **Wallpapers:** links to the pictures, one per row.
- **Change every:** how long each wallpaper stays.
- **Shuffle:** pick at random instead of in order.
