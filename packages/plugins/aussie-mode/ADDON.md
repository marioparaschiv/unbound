## :sparkle: What it does

- :arrows-rotate-center: Turns all of Discord upside down, menus, sheets and pop-ups included.
- :pointer: Taps and scrolling turn with it, so dragging the chat moves it the way your finger goes.
- :msg: Messages you send or edit go out upside down too: `please wait a moment` becomes `ʇuǝɯoɯ ɐ ʇᴉɐʍ ǝsɐǝld`. Mentions, emoji, timestamps and links stay readable.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin on to flip Discord, and off to turn it back.
