## :sparkle: What it does

- :mobile: Settings > Devices shows exactly when each session was last used, and a NEW tag on sessions from the last day.
- :pen: Tap a session to give it a name you will recognise.
- :bell: Optionally checks in the background and tells you when a new session signs in.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's BetterSessions.

## :gear: Settings

- **Check for new sessions:** checks every few minutes and shows a notice when a new device signs in.
- **Check every (minutes):** how often it checks.
