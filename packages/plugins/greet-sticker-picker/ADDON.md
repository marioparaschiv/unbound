## :sparkle: What it does

- :sticker: Tapping 'Wave to say hi!' on a new member's join message opens every greet sticker, so you send the one you want.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's GreetStickerPicker.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off to go back to how Discord behaves.
