## :sparkle: What it does

- :calendar: Long-press a message with an `.ics` invite and pick **Show calendar event** to see what, when and where, then **Add to Calendar**.
- :plus: `/event` builds an invite from a title, a start like `18:30` or `2026-12-24 19:00`, a length and a place, and attaches it to your message.
