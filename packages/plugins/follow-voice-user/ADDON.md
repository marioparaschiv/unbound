## :sparkle: What it does

- :headphones: `/follow` a friend, and you join their voice channel whenever they move to another one.
- :gear: By default it follows only while you are in voice; it can also disconnect you when they leave.
- :code-fork: A port of FollowVoiceUser for Equicord, by TheArmagan.
