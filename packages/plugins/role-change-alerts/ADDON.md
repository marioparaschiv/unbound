## :sparkle: What it does

- :shield: Tells you when a server gives you a role, and when it takes one away, naming the role and the server.
- :bell: Each notification is a toast with the server's icon.
- :circle-check: Only your own roles: changes to other members stay quiet.

## :gear: Settings

- :shield: **Notify When**: **You get a role** and **You lose a role**, both on by default.
- :bell: **Persistent notices** keeps each notification on screen until you tap **OK**. Off by default.
