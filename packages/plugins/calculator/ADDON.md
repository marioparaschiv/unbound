## :sparkle: What it does

- :percentage: Type a calculation such as `12 * 4`, `(19.99 + 4.5) * 3` or `sqrt(2) =` and a calculator button appears in the chat input.
- :pen: Tap it to swap the draft for the result, `48`, or the full calculation, `12 * 4 = 48`.

## :bullet-list: What it understands

- `+ - * / ^`, `×` and `÷`, parentheses, `%` for remainders and `15%` for percentages.
- Decimals with a dot or a comma.
- `sqrt`, `cbrt`, `abs`, `round`, `floor`, `ceil`, `min`, `max`, `log`, `ln`, `exp`, `sin`, `cos`, `tan` and the constants `pi` and `e`.
