## :sparkle: What it does

- :map: Messages with a Google Maps, Apple Maps or OpenStreetMap link to a spot, a `geo:` link, or coordinates like `48.8584, 2.2945` get a small map with a pin under them.
- :pin: Up to two maps per message, for everyone's messages, yours included.

## :shield: Privacy

- :globe: Maps load from Yandex's static maps, so Yandex sees the coordinates and your IP address when one shows.
