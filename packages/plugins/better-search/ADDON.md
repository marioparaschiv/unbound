## :sparkle: What it does

- :search: Search messages with `embedType:`, `embedProvider:`, `repliedToMessageId:`, `repliedToUserId:`, `mentionsRoleId:`, `mentionEveryone:`, `includeNsfw:`, `commandId:`, `commandName:` and `slop:`.
- :circle-info: They work like Discord's own filters such as `from:` and `has:`.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's BetterSearch.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off to go back to how Discord behaves.
