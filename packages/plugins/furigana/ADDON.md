## :sparkle: What it does

- :language: Japanese messages show the readings of their kanji in a small line under the text: 東京で勉強しています reads 東京(とうきょう)で勉強(べんきょう)しています.
- :mobile: The readings come from iOS's own Japanese dictionary, so nothing leaves your device.
- :apple: iOS only, since Android has no built-in Japanese reader.
