## :sparkle: What it does

- :dots: The three typing dots stay still instead of pulsing, so they no longer redraw every frame.
- :msgs: Covers every place Discord draws them: the typing indicator above the chat input, typing on forum posts, the typing status on avatars in your DMs, and typing in the channel list where Discord shows it.
- :circle-info: The dots drawn by **Channel Typing Indicators** and Discord's emoji or animated custom typing indicators are not Discord's typing dots, so they keep moving.
- :circle-info: The still dots look like the ones Discord shows when **Reduce Motion** is on. With Reduce Motion already on, nothing changes.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's NoTypingAnimation.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off and the dots animate again.
