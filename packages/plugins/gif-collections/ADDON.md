## :sparkle: What it does

- :folder: Sort GIFs into your own collections. Long-press a GIF in the GIF picker and tap **Add to Collection** to add it to one, take it out, or start a new collection with it.
- :grid: Each collection shows as a tile under Favorites in the GIF picker and opens in the usual GIF grid, named after the collection.
- :trash: Hold a collection in the Add to Collection sheet to delete it. Its GIFs stay in your favourites.
