## :sparkle: What it does

- :msg: Long-press a message and pick **Spell With Reactions**, type a word, and its letters go on as reactions.
- :repeat: Repeated letters use look-alikes, such as 🅰️ for a second A or ⭕ for a third O; emoji already on the message are skipped.
- :text: Letters, digits, `!` and `?`; the sheet shows the reactions before you send them, or says which letters it can't spell.
- :clock: Reactions go on one at a time so Discord accepts each.
