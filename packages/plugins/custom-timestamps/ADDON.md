## :sparkle: What it does

- :clock: Message times use your own format: one for today, such as `HH:mm`, and one for older messages, such as `ddd, D MMM YYYY HH:mm`.

## :gear: Settings

- **Today** and **Older**: patterns made of `YYYY` year, `MM` month, `MMM` month name, `DD` day, `ddd` weekday, `HH` 24-hour, `hh` 12-hour, `mm` minutes, `ss` seconds and `A` AM/PM. Text in `[brackets]` stays as written. Leave one empty to keep Discord's.
