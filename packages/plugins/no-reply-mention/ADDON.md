## :sparkle: What it does

- :bell-slash: New replies don't mention the author of the message you reply to.
- :at-sign: Keep a list of people who still get pinged when you reply to them.

## :gear: Settings

- :user-plus: **Add user ID** saves a Discord user ID to your exceptions list. IDs are 15 to 25 digits, and each one can be edited or deleted. Empty by default.
- :toggle: **Only Ping Listed Users** lets replies to users on the list mention them. Off by default, which means the list has no effect.
