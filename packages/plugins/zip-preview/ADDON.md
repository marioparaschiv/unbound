## :sparkle: What it does

- :folder: Long-press a message with a zip archive and tap **Show Files** to see every file inside and its size, without saving the archive.
- :files: Works with `.zip`, `.jar`, `.apk`, `.ipa`, `.xpi`, `.epub` and Office files, which are zip archives too.
- :circle-info: Archives up to 25 MB list; text files already open in Discord's own viewer.
