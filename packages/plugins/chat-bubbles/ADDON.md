## :sparkle: What it does

- :msgs: Wraps each message's text and media in a rounded bubble with a small margin.
- :droplet: Bubbles are translucent black on dark themes and translucent white on the light theme, and follow you when you switch.
- :arrow-rotate-anticlockwise: Stop the plugin and the bubbles go away.

## :gear: Settings

- :toggle: **Layout** picks **Fit to message**, bubbles that wrap each message's text and media, or **Full-width cards**, one card behind each whole message like Rosiecord. Fit to message by default.
- :slider: **Corner radius**, **Horizontal padding**, **Vertical padding** and **Opacity** shape the bubble. 10 pt, 8 pt, 4 pt and 40% by default. The padding sliders only apply to Fit to message.
- :color-palette: **Bubble color** sets the bubble's color. Follows the theme by default.
- :msg-user: **Your messages** gives your own messages a different color. Same as the bubble color by default.
