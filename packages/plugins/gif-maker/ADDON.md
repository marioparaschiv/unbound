## :sparkle: What it does

- :video: Long-press a message with a video and pick **Make GIF** to turn part of it into a GIF.
- :clock: Pick where it starts and how long it runs, up to 15 seconds, at 10 frames a second.
- :ruler: Pick its size, and whether to keep the whole frame or crop a centred square.
- :text: Add a caption, drawn along the bottom in white with a black outline.
- :paperclip: The GIF is attached to your message in that channel, ready to send.
- :mobile: Everything happens on your phone: the video is downloaded, its frames read, and the GIF encoded there.
