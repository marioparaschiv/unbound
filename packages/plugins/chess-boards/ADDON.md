## :sparkle: What it does

- :grid: A chess position written in FEN, alone or in a code block, gets a board image under the message, seen from the side to move.
- :link: A lichess game link gets the game's animated replay.
- :photo: Boards come from lichess and show for everyone's messages, yours included. Up to three per message.
