## :sparkle: What it does

- :eye-slash: Long-press a server and pick **Hide Server** to take it out of your server list. You stay in it.
- :folder: A folder that only holds hidden servers disappears with them.
- :code-fork: After nexpid's Hide Servers for Vendetta and Equicord's HideServers.

## :gear: Settings

- **Hidden servers:** the servers you hid. Remove one to bring it back, or add one by name or ID.
