## :sparkle: What it does

- :clock: Asks before you send a reply to a message older than a month, since a reply pings and pulls everyone back into an old conversation.
- :pen: **Keep editing** puts the message back in the chat input, with its reply, so nothing is lost.

## :gear: Settings

- :calendar: **Warn from this many days old**: 30 by default.
