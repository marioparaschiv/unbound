# Changelog

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-24

### Changed

- The store page shows screenshots of the plugin instead of a banner. The plugin works the same as before.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-19

### Fixed

- Chinese punctuation in the pinyin line is written the Latin way and spaced as in English, such as "wǒ ài nǐ, zhōng guó." instead of "wǒ ài nǐ， zhōng guó。".

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-19

### Added

- Initial release: Chinese messages show their pinyin, with tone marks, in a small line under them.
