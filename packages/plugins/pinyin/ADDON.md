## :sparkle: What it does

- :language: Messages with Chinese characters show their pinyin, with tone marks, in a small line under the text: 我爱你 reads _wǒ ài nǐ_.
- :mobile: The reading comes from the phone's own Chinese transliteration, so nothing leaves your device.
- :eye-slash: Messages with Japanese kana are left alone, since their kanji read differently.
