Who's in charge here? More User Tags labels message authors by what they can do in the server, right next to their name.

## :sparkle: What it does

- :tag: Each author gets one tag, the first enabled one that applies: Webhook, Owner, Admin, Staff, Mod or VC Mod.
- :shield: Permissions are checked with the channel's permission overwrites. Bots get no permission tags.
- :palette: The tag takes the author's role color, gradient included. Without one, each tag has its own color.
- :sparkle-3: On iOS, holographic roles get an animated sheen, left out when Reduce Motion is on.
- :msg: When Discord already shows its original poster label, the two share one badge, separated by a dot.

## :gear: Settings

- :toggle: **Webhook**, **Owner**, **Admin**, **Staff**, **Mod** and **VC Mod** turn each tag on or off. All on by default.
- :text-color: **Coloured Tags** gives each tag its own color when the author has no role color. On by default.
- :paintbrush: **Use Role Colour** colors the tag with the author's role color. On by default.
- :gradient: **Use Enhanced Role Colours** adds the secondary and tertiary colors of gradient and holographic roles. On by default.
- :award: **Use Original Poster Tag Style** styles the tag like Discord's original poster badge. Off by default.
