## :sparkle: What it does

- :leftwards_arrow_with_hook: Long-press a message and pick **Jump to Reply** to go to the first reply to it, highlighted like any jump.
- :mag: It looks through the loaded messages first, then the next 100 messages, and tells you when nobody replied.

## :gear: Settings

None. It works as soon as you turn it on.
