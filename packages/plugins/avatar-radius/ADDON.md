Circles are fine. Squircles are better. Avatar Radius draws avatars as rounded squares, as round as you like.

## :sparkle: What it does

- :square-user: Draws avatars as rounded squares instead of circles.
- :mobile: Changes Discord's native avatar views on iOS and Android.
- :arrow-rotate-anticlockwise: Turn the plugin off and avatars are circles again.

## :gear: Settings

- :slider: **Corner radius** sets how round the corners are, from 0 (square) to 64. A radius past half an avatar's size keeps it a circle. 8 by default.
