## :sparkle: What it does

- :text: Drops avatars and message headers and puts each author's name in bold before their message, like desktop's compact layout.
- :gear: Shows the name on every message, or only on the first of a group.
- :code-fork: A port of Compact Mode for Vendetta, by Cynosphere.
