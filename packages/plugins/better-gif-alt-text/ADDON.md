## :sparkle: What it does

- :eye: VoiceOver and TalkBack read a GIF's tags or file name, such as 'GIF - cat dance', instead of just 'GIF'.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's BetterGifAltText.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off to go back to how Discord behaves.
