## :sparkle: What it does

- :file-content: Tapping a PDF attachment opens it in your phone's PDF viewer instead of a browser.
- :apple: On iOS that's Quick Look: page thumbnails, search, markup and sharing.
- :android: On Android it opens in your PDF app, such as Google Drive's viewer.
- :globe: If the PDF can't be opened, it opens in the browser as before.
- :floppy-disk: PDFs download to Unbound's temporary folder, which is emptied when the plugin stops.
