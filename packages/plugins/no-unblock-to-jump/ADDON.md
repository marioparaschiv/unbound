## :sparkle: What it does

- :pointer: Jumping to a message from someone you blocked or ignored goes straight there, without the unblock prompt.
- :circle-info: Their messages stay hidden in chat the way your block settings say.
- :code-fork: A port of Vencord's NoUnblockToJump.

## :gear: Settings

None. Turn the plugin off to go back to how Discord behaves.
