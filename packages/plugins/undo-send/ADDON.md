## :sparkle: What it does

- :timer: Each message you send waits a few seconds on your phone before it goes out, with a toast that counts down.
- :undo: **Undo** in the toast stops it and puts the message back in the chat input, with its reply, so you can fix it or drop it.
- :paper-plane-2: Messages you send quickly one after another keep their order.

## :gear: Settings

- :stopwatch: **Seconds to wait**: 5 by default, from 2 to 30.

## :circle-info: Good to know

- :mobile: The wait runs on your phone. If you close Discord while a message is waiting, it is not sent; if you switch apps, it may only go out when you come back.
