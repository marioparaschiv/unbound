## :sparkle: What it does

- :keyboard: A **WPM** pill above the message box shows how fast you're typing the message you're writing.
- :stopwatch: Pauses longer than ten seconds don't count, so stepping away doesn't drag the number down.
- :star: Each message you send of 15 characters or more counts toward your average and best; `/typing-speed` shows them.

## :gear: Settings

- **Show while typing:** the live pill.
- **Reset statistics:** forget your average and best.
