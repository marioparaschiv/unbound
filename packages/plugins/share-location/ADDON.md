## :sparkle: What it does

- :pin: A **Share location** button in the chat input adds a Google Maps link to where you are to your message. Nothing is sent until you send it.
- :map: With Map Previews on, the link shows as a map.

## :shield: Privacy

- :globe: iOS asks for permission the first time. Your location is read only when you tap the button and goes nowhere but your message.
- :apple: iOS only.
