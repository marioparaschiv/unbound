## :sparkle: What it does

- :circle-user: Server icons round again, with the selected server as a rounded square like older Discord, or round too.
- :brush: Round server bar buttons, chat bar buttons, a pill You bar and message box, and a pill Quests, Shop and Settings bar.
- :gear: Folders, channel rows, inputs and buttons fully round, and cards and settings rows back to their older corners.
- :circle-info: Every part has its own switch. Lists built before a change keep their shapes until Discord restarts.
- :code-fork: A port of bleelblep's Fuck Squares for Revenge.
