## :sparkle: What it does

- :tag: Channels in the channel list get small badges: NSFW, Locked and Rules.
- :text: Optionally, every channel also names its type, such as Text, News or Forum.
- :code-fork: A port of ChannelBadges for Equicord, by creations and thororen.
