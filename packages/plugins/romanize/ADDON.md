## :sparkle: What it does

- :sparkle: Long-press a message written in another script and pick **Romanize** to read it in Latin letters, with a button to copy it.
- :language: Korean follows the Revised Romanization of Korean, the official system since 2000, with its sound changes: 신라 reads _silla_, 종로 _jongno_ and 감사합니다 _gamsahamnida_.
- :globe: Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Thai, Devanagari, Japanese and Chinese use the phone's own transliteration.
