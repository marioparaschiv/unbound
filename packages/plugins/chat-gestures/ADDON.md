## :sparkle: What it does

- :pointer: Pick what a double tap, a triple tap and a swipe do on a message: reply (or edit your own), react with an emoji you choose, forward, save for later, copy the text or delete your own message.
- :circle-info: Each gesture can keep Discord's default. Deleting always asks first.

## :gear: Settings

- **Double tap**, **Triple tap** and **Swipe:** the action for each. A triple tap needs a short wait after a double tap, so it is off by default.
- **Reaction emoji:** the emoji the react action uses, unicode or a custom `<:name:id>`.
