Role colors shouldn't stop at the member list. Role Color Everywhere paints names and mentions with their role colors across Discord.

## :sparkle: What it does

- :at-sign: Colors mentions in messages, embeds, reply previews and the chat input.
- :users: Colors names in the typing indicator, reaction details, poll results and voice channels, plus member list role headers.
- :palette: Roles with two or three colors paint the name one character at a time.
- :brush: Can tint each message's text with its author's role color.

## :gear: Settings

Turn each place on or off. All are on by default except **Color Message Content**, which comes with a **Message color intensity** slider set to 30%.
