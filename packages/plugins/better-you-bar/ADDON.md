## :sparkle: What it does

- :star: A Favorites button in the You bar opens your favorite channels.
- :gear: A Settings button in the You bar opens User Settings.
- :bookmark: With **Favorites past the limit**, favorite channels you add stay on this device with no limit.
- :code-fork: A port of BetterYouBar for Revenge, by j and Benjii.
