## :sparkle: What it does

- :msg-check: Long-press a message and pick **Follow Up Later** to keep it on a list of things you still need to answer, apart from unread messages.
- :bullet-list: Open the list from **Follow-Ups** in the same menu, or with `/follow-ups`. Tap an entry to jump to its message; long-press it to mark it done.
- :msg-arrow-right: Replying to a message clears its follow-up, so the list only holds what is still open.
- :floppy-disk: The list stays on your phone and survives restarts.

## :gear: Settings

- :msg-check: **Done when you reply**: on by default.
