## :sparkle: What it does

- :photo: `/xkcd` opens the latest comic, with its title, date and alt text.
- :magnifier: `/xkcd 927` opens a comic by number, `/xkcd random` a random one, and `/xkcd standards` searches titles.
- :arrow-right: Step through comics with **Previous**, **Next** and **Random**.
- :share-up-right: **Share to Chat** sends the comic's link to the chat you're in, where Discord embeds it.
