## :sparkle: What it does

- :photo: Redraws 552 of Discord's icons with [Nucleo](https://nucleoapp.com) Micro Bold, outline by default and filled where Discord's is.
- :palette: Covers the redesigned icons and the older coloured ones, in tabs, settings, menus and chat.
- :file: Brand logos the set does not draw keep Discord's own.
