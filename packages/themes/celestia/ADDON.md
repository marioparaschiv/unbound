## :sparkle: What it does

- :palette: Plum and coral over an anime sunset beneath a ringed planet.
- :photo: Adds a matching chat background, which you can change or turn off in Design → Chat.
- :brush: Recolours chat, the server and channel lists, sheets, menus, buttons, switches, mentions and reactions.
