## :sparkle: What it does

- :palette: A light take on Midnight Mobile.
- :photo: Adds a matching chat background, which you can change or turn off in Design → Chat.
- :brush: Recolours chat, the server and channel lists, sheets, menus, buttons, switches, mentions and reactions.
- :sparkle: Based on [Midnight](https://github.com/refact0r/midnight-discord) by refact0r.
- :heart: Made by dariussbezy for Valenced, from [dariussbezy's Plugins](https://github.com/dariussbezy/dariussbezy-Plugins).
