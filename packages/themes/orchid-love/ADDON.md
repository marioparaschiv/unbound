## :sparkle: What it does

- :palette: Orchid Love, a dark theme by Vodka Martini.
- :photo: Adds a matching chat background, which you can change or turn off in Design → Chat.
- :brush: Recolours chat, the server and channel lists, sheets, menus, buttons, switches, mentions and reactions.
- :heart: Made by Vodka Martini for Vendetta ([VodkaXMartini/VendettaTheme](https://github.com/VodkaXMartini/VendettaTheme)).
- :heart: Updated to Discord's current colour tokens by Rosie for Valenced, from [Rosie's Plugs](https://code.is-your.app/SheWhoReignsSupreme/valenced-plugins).
