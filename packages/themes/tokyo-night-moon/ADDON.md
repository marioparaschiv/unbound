## :sparkle: What it does

- :palette: Tokyo Night Moon: softer contrast with warmer accents.
- :photo: Adds a matching chat background, which you can change or turn off in Design → Chat.
- :brush: Recolours chat, the server and channel lists, sheets, menus, buttons, switches, mentions and reactions.
- :sparkle: Based on the [Tokyo Night](https://github.com/enkia/tokyo-night-vscode-theme) palette by enkia.
- :heart: Made by Benjii for Valenced, from [Benjii's Plugins / Themes](https://github.com/Accentaro/Valenced-Plugins), licensed GPL-3.0.
