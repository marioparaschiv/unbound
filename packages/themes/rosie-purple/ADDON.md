## :sparkle: What it does

- :palette: Dark purple-grey backgrounds with lilac headings and accents.
- :brush: Recolours chat, the server and channel lists, sheets, menus, buttons, switches, mentions and reactions.
- :heart: Ported from [acquitelol/unbound-themes](https://github.com/acquitelol/unbound-themes) to Discord's current colour tokens.
