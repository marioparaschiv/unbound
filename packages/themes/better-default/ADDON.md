## :sparkle: What it does

- :palette: Original theme made by xRqz3r for AliucordKT, ported to RN by Vodka Martini.
- :brush: Recolours chat, the server and channel lists, sheets, menus, buttons, switches, mentions and reactions.
- :heart: Made by xRqz3r and Vodka Martini for Vendetta ([VodkaXMartini/VendettaTheme](https://github.com/VodkaXMartini/VendettaTheme)). Originally by xRqz3r for AliucordKT.
- :heart: Updated to Discord's current colour tokens by Rosie for Valenced, from [Rosie's Plugs](https://code.is-your.app/SheWhoReignsSupreme/valenced-plugins).
